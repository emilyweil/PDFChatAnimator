Ways to download your texts

Sync and Export via Mac (Best Free Option if You Own a Mac)

  • Enable message syncing by opening the Messages app on your iPhone, going to settings, and enabling iCloud sync for messages.
  • Open the Messages app on your Mac logged into the same Apple ID.
  • Select the conversation you want to download and let it fully load.
  • Click File > Print from the top menu bar.
  • Click the PDF dropdown menu in the print dialogue and choose Save as PDF.

Forward Small Threads via Email (No Computer Needed)

  • Open the Messages app on your iPhone and go to the target conversation.
  • Touch and hold a message bubble, then tap More...
  • Tap the check circles to select the messages you wish to save.
  • Tap the share or forward arrow icon and enter your own email address to send it.

Use Third-Party Desktop Software (Best for Large History or Windows PCs)

  • Download and install a specialized manager like iMazing, Decipher TextMessage, or TouchCopy on your PC or Mac.
  • Connect your iPhone to your computer using a USB cable and tap Trust This Computer.
  • Open the software, navigate to the Messages tab, and select the threads or contacts you want to archive.
  • Export and save the files directly to your computer as a PDF, HTML, CSV, or text file.
